The following is a general guide for initial enquiries in India. Actual products, lender participation, rates, fees, documents and approval criteria vary by institution, applicant profile and location.

PERSONAL FINANCE

Personal Loan

Usually an unsecured loan for eligible salaried or self-employed applicants.

Typical eligibility factors

  • Age generally 21–60/65 years, depending on lender
  • Stable employment or verifiable business income
  • Acceptable credit history and repayment capacity
  • Minimum income and work/business vintage as set by lender

Common documents

  • PAN and identity/address proof
  • Recent salary slips or income proof
  • Usually 3–6 months bank statements
  • For self-employed: ITR, computation and business proof
VEHICLE FINANCE

Car Loan

Finance for a new or used car, subject to vehicle, borrower and lender criteria.

Typical eligibility factors

  • Minimum age and income as per lender policy
  • Stable salary or business income
  • Credit score, existing EMIs and repayment capacity
  • Vehicle age, model, valuation and down payment for used cars

Common documents

  • PAN, identity and address proof
  • Salary slips / ITR and bank statements
  • Employment or business proof
  • Vehicle quotation; RC and valuation documents for used cars
PROPERTY FINANCE

Home Loan

Funding to purchase, construct, extend or renovate a residential property, depending on product.

Typical eligibility factors

  • Age, income, employment/business stability
  • Credit profile, FOIR and repayment capacity
  • Property title, legal and technical due diligence
  • Margin contribution/down payment and loan-to-value limits

Common documents

  • KYC, PAN and photographs
  • Salary slips / ITR, Form 16 where applicable
  • Bank statements and employment/business proof
  • Agreement for sale, title chain, approved plan and property tax/other property papers as applicable
CAREER & EDUCATION

Education Loan

Funding for eligible higher education courses in India or abroad; co-applicant and collateral requirements may apply.

Typical eligibility factors

  • Admission to a recognized institution/course
  • Academic record and course eligibility
  • Co-applicant income and credit profile
  • Collateral/security based on loan amount and lender scheme

Common documents

  • Student and co-applicant KYC
  • Admission letter, fee schedule and academic records
  • Co-applicant income proof and bank statements
  • Collateral/property documents if required
MSME & ENTERPRISE

Business / MSME Loan

Finance for working capital, expansion, equipment, business needs or eligible government-backed schemes.

Typical eligibility factors

  • Business vintage, turnover and cash flows
  • Banking conduct, credit history and existing obligations
  • GST, ITR and financial performance where applicable
  • Promoter contribution, security or guarantee if required

Common documents

  • Entity PAN, constitution and KYC of promoters
  • Udyam, GST and business registration where applicable
  • Bank statements, ITR, audited financials and GST returns
  • Project report, quotations, projections and collateral papers if applicable
SECURED FINANCE

Loan Against Property (LAP)

A secured loan against eligible residential or commercial property.

Typical eligibility factors

  • Verifiable income and repayment capacity
  • Property title, valuation and legal/technical checks
  • Credit history and existing obligations
  • Property type, location and lender loan-to-value policy

Common documents

  • KYC and income proof (salary or business)
  • Bank statements, ITR and financials
  • Title deeds, chain documents and property tax receipts
  • Approved plan and other property papers requested by lender
PROPERTY-BACKED

Loan Against Securities

Credit against eligible shares, mutual fund units, bonds or other accepted securities.

Typical eligibility factors

  • Eligible securities held with an accepted depository/issuer
  • Available collateral value after applicable haircut
  • Borrower KYC and lender credit policy
  • Ongoing maintenance of collateral cover

Common documents

  • PAN, identity and address proof
  • Demat/holding statement or investment proof
  • Bank details and mandate/pledge documentation
  • Income and other documents if requested
